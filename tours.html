Livingstone Travel Tips & Excursion Advice

Practical advice from our local team to help you prepare for the best Zambian adventure.

Victoria Falls Water Levels

High Water (Feb – July): Peak roaring volume, thunderous spray and vibrant rainbows. Waterproof poncho is essential!

Low Water (Aug – Jan): Clearer views of the rock face and gorge; optimal season for Devil's Pool and Livingstone Island trips.

Zambia KAZA UniVisa

Visiting both Zambia and Zimbabwe or doing the Chobe day trip? Request the KAZA UniVisa ($50 USD) on arrival at Livingstone Airport (LVI) for seamless multi-entry across both nations.

What to Bring on Tours

Pack light quick-drying clothing, secure walking shoes with grip, sunscreen, mosquito repellent, passport (for border trips), and a waterproof pouch for phones and cameras.
