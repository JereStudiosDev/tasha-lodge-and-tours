Our Journey & Philosophy

Where attentive service meets the natural wonder of Mosi-oa-Tunya.

Founded in 2013 in the leafy residential enclave of Highlands, Livingstone, Tasha Lodge & Tours was established with a singular vision: to craft a welcoming sanctuary where international adventurers and business executives alike feel genuinely at home in Zambia.

Livingstone is celebrated worldwide as Africa's adventure capital and the proud home of the magnificent Victoria Falls (Mosi-oa-Tunya, The Smoke That Thunders). While days in Livingstone are filled with heart-pounding rapids, safari encounters, and helicopter flights, evenings call for tranquil serenity. At Tasha Lodge, we offer that vital balance — peaceful landscaped gardens, an inviting pool, air-conditioned rooms, and a restful atmosphere shielded from crowded city commotion.

Over the past decade, our locally rooted team has hosted thousands of satisfied guests from across the globe. We pride ourselves on transparent, straightforward hospitality: genuine Zambian warmth, meticulous room cleanliness, secure private parking, and curated safari excursions managed by licensed native guides.

Our Four Core Pillars

The values that define every guest experience at Tasha Lodge & Tours.

1. Executive Comfort

Restorative sleep is paramount. Every room is outfitted with whisper-quiet air conditioning, private en-suite bathrooms, crisp white linens, and complimentary high-speed Wi-Fi.

2. Authentic Local Tours

We do not just host; we guide. Our in-house tour operations ensure direct access to Victoria Falls walks, Mukuni Big 5 wildlife encounters, and Zambezi river sunset cruises.

3. Secure & Private Grounds

Relax with complete peace of mind. Our lodge is situated in a quiet residential area featuring fully enclosed boundary walls, gated access, 24/7 security, and safe on-site parking.

4. Direct Personalized Care

As an owner-operated lodge, we cater directly to your itinerary. From tailored airport pickups to early-morning safari breakfast boxes, we make every detail effortless.
